What we do

Services

From a new septic system to a load of gravel, one local crew handles the whole job.

Specialty

Septic installation

New septic tanks and systems for homes, cottages and farms, plus replacement of old or failing systems.

Excavation

Digging for basements, foundations, additions and garages, with backfill once the walls are up.

Trucking & hauling

Gravel, fill, sand and topsoil delivered, and excess dirt and debris hauled away.

Grading & drainage

Levelling yards and building pads and fixing wet spots so water runs where it should.

Driveways & laneways

New gravel driveways and farm lanes built on a solid base, or old ones rebuilt.

Trenching

Trenches for water lines, hydro, gas and other services to the house or barn.
